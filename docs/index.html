Selector Preference

Five PrismaUI overlay sketches for the same settings file. Click through them in the browser; nothing here talks to the game. Notch choices stick in this browser so you can hop layouts and keep the same taste. Pick a shape, or say what to steal from more than one.

Mockup 1

Compact card

Small panel parked on the right, SkyUI-adjacent. Fast to open while testing in the Bannered Mare. Dense, not pretty.
Mockup 2

Word notches

No slider track. Each preference is a row of named buttons, because the packets are discrete, not 0–100 prose.
Mockup 3

Situation columns

Your turn / room continues / idle as three cards. Each column shows what 0 means and which sliders feed that letter.
Mockup 4

Letter preview

Controls on the left, fake compiled letter on the right. Change a notch or a scene and watch the packet swap. Teaching tool more than HUD.
Mockup 5

Thin HUD

Bottom-left strip, low chrome, meant to stay up while you talk. Closest to “hotkey, tweak, keep playing.”
